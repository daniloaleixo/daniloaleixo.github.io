About me

I spent 10 years shipping software the traditional way. Now at numa, I'm changing how we build it, with AI agents involved in every step from ticket to production.

Most of my work is building what makes the agents reliable. That includes test and eval pipelines, LLM deployments on AWS, and workflows that take the repetitive work off engineers' plates. I still own the architecture of our pricing platform, so I see every day where AI helps in production and where it doesn't.

Before numa, I worked in large corporations, consulted for major banks and fintechs, and built products at startups and scale-ups of every stage.

If you're working on agentic engineering or bringing AI into a tech team, feel free to reach out. I also enjoy mentoring.
